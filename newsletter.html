Thanksgiving and Holiday Events in Boca Raton and Delray Beach

The season starts earlier here than most people expect. Boca lights its tree on November 21, five days before Thanksgiving, and Delray raises its 100-foot tree on December 1. Between them: a Thanksgiving morning Turkey Trot, two downtown parades on back-to-back nights, nightly snowfall at CityPlace, a community Hanukkah celebration, and Sandi — the 700-ton sand tree — back on the West Palm waterfront. Eight events, all within about half an hour of the Polo Club gates.

The Author's Take · Opinion
My opinion, plainly labeled as opinion: something changed inside the gates this week. We went from five homes available to ten in five days — five new listings, three of them on the same day. Months of inventory moved from roughly 0.7 to 1.3. The Polo Club is still the tightest market of the twenty-one communities we track, but only just: ten against seventy-nine sales, where Boca West CC sits at 28 against 219. Five days ago that lead was comfortable. I read this as sellers choosing to list before the new year rather than any softening in demand — but if you have been waiting for choice in the Polo Club, this is the most choice we have had all year, and it has arrived in the quietest buying season.
