Thanksgiving and Holiday Events in Boca Raton and Delray Beach

Tree lightings, a Thanksgiving morning Turkey Trot, two downtown parades, two Chanukah celebrations and the return of Sandi — what to put on the calendar this season.
By Samantha Gornstein · The Edmund Bogen Team at Douglas Elliman Real Estate · November 16, 2026

Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26, and for Polo Club residents hosting family this year, there's plenty to do together before and after the big meal. Boca Raton lights its holiday tree the weekend before Thanksgiving, West Boca's annual Turkey Trot gives families a reason to get outside on Thursday morning, and CityPlace has an evening snowfall for anyone looking to get out of the house over the long weekend.

In December, the celebrations move to Atlantic Avenue and the West Palm Beach waterfront, with two downtown parades, Delray Beach's 100-foot Christmas tree, two Chanukah gatherings and the return of Sandi, West Palm Beach's Christmas tree made of sand. Here's what to put on the calendar.

November and Thanksgiving Weekend

Boca Raton Holiday Tree Lighting

Saturday, November 21 · 6–10 p.m. · Mizner Park Amphitheater

Mizner Park Amphitheater hosts Boca Raton's free kickoff to the holiday season on the Saturday before Thanksgiving. The evening includes live music, a holiday market, children's crafts and photos with Santa. Food and drinks will be available for purchase, so it's easy to make an evening of it with family or friends. The interior roads at Mizner Park close to vehicle traffic at 5 p.m., an hour before festivities begin.

City of Boca Raton event details →

Levis JCC Thanksgiving Turkey Trot in West Boca

Thursday, November 26 · 7:30 a.m. · Sunset Cove Amphitheater

If your household likes to get moving before the turkey goes in the oven, head to Sunset Cove Amphitheater for the 18th annual Levis JCC Turkey Trot. The Thanksgiving morning event includes a 5K run or walk and a separate one-mile youth fun run. Gates open at 6 a.m., and the 5K starts at 7:30 a.m., leaving much of the day free for Thanksgiving plans. Proceeds benefit Levis JCC programs and services for local children and families.

Turkey Trot and registration details →

Thanksgiving Weekend Snowfall at CityPlace

Nightly · 6:30, 7:30 and 8:30 p.m. · West Palm Beach

For an outing with out-of-town guests, pair dinner in downtown West Palm Beach with a stop in the CityPlace square. With three scheduled snowfalls each evening, families with young children can catch the first one, while anyone lingering over dinner can aim for a later show. The snowfall continues through December 31, weather permitting.

CityPlace snowfall schedule →

December Holiday Events

Delray Beach's 100-Foot Christmas Tree Lighting and Yuletide Street Fair

Tuesday, December 1 · 6–9 p.m. · Old School Square

Delray Beach opens December with one of its best-known traditions. The street fair spreads across Old School Square, Atlantic Avenue and Pineapple Grove, with vendors, musical performances and children's crafts. Food trucks on Swinton Avenue make it easy to grab dinner while you explore. Santa is scheduled to light the tree at 7 p.m., so there's time to browse before the countdown or stay afterward to see downtown lit up.

Delray Beach tree lighting and street fair details →

Boca Raton Holiday Street Parade

Wednesday, December 2 · 7:30–9 p.m. · Downtown Boca Raton

This year's parade theme is "Boca by the Beach," a fitting backdrop for an evening of decorated floats, music and dancing from local community groups. Anyone who has watched the parade from the same spot for years should check the city's event page before heading out. The city expects changes to the route because of roadwork on Federal Highway.

Boca Raton Holiday Street Parade updates →

Holiday in Paradise and the Lighting of Sandi

Thursday, December 3 · 6–10 p.m. · West Palm Beach Waterfront

West Palm Beach puts its own spin on the Christmas tree with Sandi, the downtown waterfront's signature sand sculpture. The free opening celebration features live entertainment and Sandi's first lighting of the season. It also marks the start of nightly music and light shows, so you can plan a waterfront stroll on another December evening if opening night fills up your calendar.

West Palm Beach Holiday in Paradise schedule →

Downtown Delray Beach Chanukah Festival and Menorah Lighting

Sunday, December 6 · 6–9 p.m. · Old School Square, Delray Beach

Five days after the 100-foot tree goes up on the same lawn, Old School Square hosts Downtown Delray Beach's Chanukah festival and menorah lighting. The gathering is at 51 North Swinton Avenue, a short walk from Atlantic Avenue, so it pairs easily with dinner downtown.

Downtown Delray Beach events calendar →

Community Hanukkah Celebration at CityPlace

Friday, December 11 · West Palm Beach

Palm Beach Synagogue West brings its community Hanukkah celebration to CityPlace with a menorah lighting, live music and face painting. Families can enjoy latkes, doughnuts and gelt as part of the gathering. If you're bringing children, you can also plan your visit around one of CityPlace's evening snowfalls.

CityPlace holiday calendar →

Delray Beach Holiday Parade

Saturday, December 12 · 6–9 p.m. · Atlantic Avenue

The following evening, Delray Beach takes the celebration back to Atlantic Avenue. Floats, marching bands and dance teams are scheduled to travel from the Intracoastal Bridge to Northwest Fifth Avenue. For Polo Club families looking for a Saturday outing with grandparents or visiting relatives, make an early dinner reservation downtown and leave time to find a spot along the route.

One of the pleasures of living at The Polo Club is having both Boca Raton and Delray Beach close enough for a spontaneous evening out, with the West Palm Beach waterfront an option when you have a little more time. If family is visiting this season, take them to a tradition you already love and pick one new event to try together.

Wishing our Polo Club neighbors a happy Thanksgiving and a wonderful holiday season.
